# FHIR-56671: Number figures and give them stable anchors so they can be cross-referenced

## Description

Figures lack numbers and stable anchors. Cross-references can't survive across pages or versions, and review feedback suffers ("the diagram on the page about ..." instead of "Figure 2.1").

Proposed resolution:

Number figures (e.g., "Figure 2.1") and give them stable anchors (e.g., #fig-2-1) for inline reference. May require a template-level change in the FHIR IG publisher.
