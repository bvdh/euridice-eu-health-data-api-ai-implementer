# FHIR-56645: On-demand FHIR Documents should be explicitly supported; soften the "immutable snapshot" framing

## Description

fhir-documents-vs-resources.html opens with:

FHIR Documents are complete, immutable snapshots of clinical information at a point in time.

This implies a registry-style storage model: documents persisted once, versioned, never re-rendered. Many EHRs assemble Documents on demand from operational data and do not store pre-rendered Bundles. The current wording reads as a persistence/versioning SHALL the IG does not intend.

MHD already supports on-demand documents. Per [ITI-67 §3.67.4.1.2.6 Document Location](https://profiles.ihe.net/ITI/MHD/ITI-67.html#236742211-document-location):

An on-demand DocumentReference is identified by its lack of both content.attachment.hash and content.attachment.size. The content.attachment.url may resolve to any endpoint that produces a valid Document Bundle.

Operation invocations such as Patient/[id]/$summary qualify; servers can return a fresh Bundle each time the URL is dereferenced.

Imaging reports are also not "summaries in a moment" the way a Patient Summary is. Addenda, key images, and communication of critical results can change after the radiologist signs the report without forcing re-attestation.

Proposed resolution:

fhir-documents-vs-resources.html (line 5) — replace the immutable-snapshot opener with on-demand-or-persisted framing:

FHIR Documents are clinical-report representations of a patient's health information at a defined moment, expressed as FHIR Bundles with type="document" containing a Composition resource. Documents may be persisted or assembled on demand; both are first-class patterns for this IG. Servers choosing on-demand assembly are not required to version, history, or persist past renderings.

document-exchange.html — add an On-Demand Documents subsection citing ITI-67's absent-hash/size convention and noting that content.attachment.url may invoke an operation (e.g., Patient/[id]/$summary).

priority-area-eps.html — change the $summary mention from informational to permitted: "Servers MAY support Patient/[id]/$summary as the assembly mechanism; clients SHOULD discover support via the CapabilityStatement."

priority-area-imaging-report.html — note that imaging reports differ from summaries: addenda, key images, and critical-result communications may attach to the same DocumentReference without re-attestation.

Sweep the IG for SHALL/SHOULD language that implies persistence or versioning (e.g., status = "current" rules that assume latest-of-multiple) and soften where persistence isn't actually mandated.
