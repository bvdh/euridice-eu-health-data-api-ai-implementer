# FHIR-56618: Revise identifier usage

## Description

The statement:

“Both Provider and Consumer SHALL support the identifier parameter for patient search.”

does not fully align with cross-border constraints and identification practices:

	- In cross-border scenarios, the Provider must support identifier-based search

	- However, the Consumer may not always be able to provide a single identifier, as not all countries use a national patient identifier and may rely on a set of agreed identification attributes

 

Proposed Change:

Update the requirement to differentiate responsibilities, e.g.:

“The Provider SHALL support the identifier parameter for patient search.

The Consumer SHOULD support the identifier parameter where a trusted identifier is available.”

## Resolution

- Status: Resolved - change required
- Resolution: Not Persuasive with Modification
- Resolution Vote: Mattias Colliander / Charles Parisot: 6-0-1
- Vote Date: 2026-08-12

Based on the agreement reached during the HL7 EU API Call on June 10th (see [concept page](https://confluence.hl7.org/x/mlgOGw)) the term “system SHALL support a parameter XY” means a general capability and not that this parameter XY must be present at every query. The wording in the specification will be updated to make this clearer. Therefore, the SHALL requirement to support identifier for both actors can remain.
