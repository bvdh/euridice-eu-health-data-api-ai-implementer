# FHIR-56459: Missing healthcare providers in Audience

## Description

In paragraph 1.3 "Audience" ([Home - EU Health Data API v1.0.0-ballot](https://hl7.eu/fhir/health-data-api/1.0.0-ballot/en/index.html)).

Healthcare providers (HCP) should also be in the list. They have some obligations and they will rely on their EHR systems suppliers to implement them. A certification logo is not enough. These organizations need to understand the use cases and how they can be implemented, in regard to the rules defined by the country of treatment (obligation to go through a national of regional repositories, direct HCP to HCP communication ...). Then they will be able to check of the Interoperability Component of their suppliers really fit their needs.
