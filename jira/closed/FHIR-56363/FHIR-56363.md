### JoshPriebe — 2026-05-13

Implementation details:

References on [regulatoryAnchors.html](https://build.fhir.org/ig/euridice-org/eu-health-data-api/en/regulatoryAnchors.html) (and others) to §2.1 / §2.2 are ambiguous; they read as IG section numbers but refer to the EHDS regulation.

Proposed fix: For references to the regulation; prefix all occurrences with "EHDS <Article/Annex>" and deeplink where possible to the regulation section: [EHDS Annex II §2.1](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2025/327/oj/eng#anx_II)

EHDS References to update:

	- [EHDS Regulation](https://build.fhir.org/ig/euridice-org/eu-health-data-api/en/regulatoryAnchors.html#ehds-regulation) — bullet list: "§2.1: SHALL provide an interface..." / "§2.2: SHALL be able to receive..."

	- [Xt-EHR Deliverable 5.1](https://build.fhir.org/ig/euridice-org/eu-health-data-api/en/regulatoryAnchors.html#xt-ehr-deliverable-51) — prose: "interpreted 2.1 and 2.2 as two sides of..."; table column: "§2.1 provide interface enabling access" / "§2.2 be able to receive"

	- [§2.1: Provide Access](https://build.fhir.org/ig/euridice-org/eu-health-data-api/en/regulatoryAnchors.html#21-provide-access) — section header + prose: "satisfy §2.1" / "delegate their §2.1 obligations"

	- [§2.2: Receive Data](https://build.fhir.org/ig/euridice-org/eu-health-data-api/en/regulatoryAnchors.html#22-receive-data) — section header + prose: "satisfy §2.2" / "not required to satisfy §2.2"

	- [index.html — Regulatory Basis](https://build.fhir.org/ig/euridice-org/eu-health-data-api/en/index.html#regulatory-basis) — bullet list: "§2.1: SHALL provide an interface..." / "§2.2: SHALL be able to receive..."

Internal IG cross-references use section titles, not §x.x paragraph numbers — pairs with [FHIR-56364](https://jira.hl7.org/browse/FHIR-56364) (stable anchors). In most cases this is already done; here's one:

	- Actor deep links - [actors.html](https://build.fhir.org/ig/euridice-org/eu-health-data-api/en/actors.html) defines individual actors with bold text, not headings, so no anchors are generated. Internal links don't resolve. Fix: add explicit anchor definition in actors.md, ref IHE IG implementations.

Also found an external MHD link to be improved

	- [document-exchange.html — Document Publication](https://build.fhir.org/ig/euridice-org/eu-health-data-api/en/document-exchange.html) — links to IHE MHD via auto-generated anchor #231054122-patient-identity; anchor is numeric/auto-generated and will break if IHE restructures the page. Fix: link to the section by title

No references are currently made to Xt-EHR document sections; just overall work packages. No changes required.
