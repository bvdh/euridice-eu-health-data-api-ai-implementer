# FHIR-56709: Revise Section 4.1.7

## Description

Section 4.1.7

 

The sentence:

“Multiple documents may be returned if patient has been seen multiple times”

is not appropriate for the Patient Summary (PS) use case:

	- In the EU cross-border context, typically only one active Patient Summary exists per patient

	- The Patient Summary is not encounter-based and is not created per visit

	- It is a continuously updated document, not a collection of encounter reports

Proposed Change:

Revise the statement to reflect the nature of the Patient Summary,

## Resolution

- Status: Resolved - change required
- Resolution: Persuasive
- Resolution Vote: 9-0-0
- Vote Date: 2026-10-21

Adopt Reinhard's proposed replacement sentence. Replace "Multiple documents may be returned if patient has been seen multiple times" with: "A single or multiple Patient Summary documents might be returned depending on the various [use cases or national infrastructures](https://build.fhir.org/ig/euridice-org/eu-health-data-api/en/implementation.html)." Multiple Patient Summaries may occur across different infrastructures; how a consumer selects or reconciles them is outside the scope of this API IG. The agreed action is to use the replacement sentence, rather than retain the alternative of deleting the sentence.
