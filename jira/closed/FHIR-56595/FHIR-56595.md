# FHIR-56595: Move Authors to About

## Description

To have a better alignment between the various HL7 Europe IGs, the suggestion is to move the information about Authors to the About chapter.

Furthermore, the majority of the other HL7 EU IGs acknowledge the authors in general way, without listing persons, maybe this could be an approach to follow.

See for example the other IGs:

	- [https://hl7.eu/fhir/base/authors.html](https://hl7.eu/fhir/base/authors.html)

	- [https://hl7.eu/fhir/hdr/contributors.html](https://hl7.eu/fhir/hdr/contributors.html)
