# FHIR-56155: Requiring Patient read before reading documents is unnecessary and will likely break legal compliance

## Description

The addition of Patient lookup seems unnecessary for document search, and at least not well explored on its impact.

What happens if systems cannot expose the patient object? are they non-compliant (I believe the spec says yes).

what to do for logical references? And pseudo/anonymised patients?

Volunteering this scope addition doesn't seem to make sense in something that would normally be candidate for a core specification and as such rather conservative and careful to ensure adoption.

## Resolution

- Status: Applied
- Resolution: Persuasive
- Resolution Vote: Mattias Colliander / Charles Parisot: 6-0-1
- Vote Date: 2026-08-12

As already stated in the comments patient lookup functions should not be a hard requirement for all Access Provider systems. To elaborate this further, an information disclaimer will be added to the top of the Patient Lookup page, explaining that there will be different architectures and strategies in the member states of even within a healthcare provider organization, leading to the situation that - not every access provider system has to provide a Patient Lookup API, it depends on the national/regional/local architectures which components have to provide it or if it is needed at all - depending which patient identifier(s) are used and in which form - an identifier conversion mechanism might be needed, which could even result in the need of doing more than one patient lookup transaction - or a patient lookup transaction might not be needed at all

### Reinhard Egelkraut — 2026-05-17

If I may jump in here - actually there are mixed messages in the ballot version of the spec

On the "Actor and Transactions" overview page, chapter 2.2.2. states in the sequence diagram that for both the document publish and the document query transaction a Patient Lookup is required.

see [https://hl7.eu/fhir/health-data-api/1.0.0-ballot/en/actors.html#document-exchange](https://hl7.eu/fhir/health-data-api/1.0.0-ballot/en/actors.html#document-exchange) 

 

Whereas on the detailed "Functional Requirements" page for "Document Exchange", it clearly says that patient lookup transaction is not required, just an option, at least for the publication process. The requirement is that the corresponding patient is identified, how the identification information was obtained depends on the architecture and which components are available for that (e.g. EHR System that supports both document information and patient information or not, availability of a separate MPI, extracting an identifier from the EUDI Wallet information during authentication, etc.).

see [https://hl7.eu/fhir/health-data-api/1.0.0-ballot/en/document-exchange.html#patient-identity-in-document-publication](https://hl7.eu/fhir/health-data-api/1.0.0-ballot/en/document-exchange.html#patient-identity-in-document-publication)
