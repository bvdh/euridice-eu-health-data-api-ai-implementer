# FHIR-56654: Patient matching: establish Patient.$match as the primary mechanism; require demographics in addition to identifier; permit `onlyCertainMatch` semantics

## Description

The IG requires Patient.Search (ITI-78) with identifier as a SHALL parameter and lists Patient.$match (ITI-119) as MAY. This framing implies that a single shared business identifier suffices for safe cross-organization patient matching.

Real-world experience shows identifier-only matching causes patient-safety incidents when identifiers are duplicated, mistyped, shared across persons, or quality-impaired (the Swedish personnummer error rate is the common example). Identifier matching is necessary but not sufficient; demographics are needed alongside.

Epic's patient matching guidelines, developed across decades of operating an EMPI across health systems, treat a pair as a confident match only when ALL of the following hold:

	- Exact or near-exact name match

	- Exact sex/gender match

	- Exact DOB match

	- At least one additional attribute: full address, exact phone, exact email, government-issued identifier, or MRN

A state-issued identifier does not remove the need to match on demographics. Given the patient-safety risk of mismatched identity, supplementing matched national identifiers is always appropriate.

Proposed resolution:

1. Establish Patient.$match (ITI-119) as the primary matching mechanism in this IG.

2. Recommend (or require) consumers supply demographics — name, sex, DOB — alongside identifiers.

3. Permit servers to behave as if onlyCertainMatch=true (returning only high-confidence matches) without requiring the consumer to send the parameter.

4. Revisit the SHALL on Patient.Search-with-identifier-required: the safety case for identifier-only lookup is weaker than the IG implies, and [FHIR-56155](https://jira.hl7.org/browse/FHIR-56155) and [FHIR-56304](https://jira.hl7.org/browse/FHIR-56304) raise architectural concerns about requiring patient lookup at all.

This ticket overlaps with [FHIR-56155](https://jira.hl7.org/browse/FHIR-56155) (Jose: patient read before documents unnecessary), [FHIR-56304](https://jira.hl7.org/browse/FHIR-56304) (Johan: chained identifier search formal option), [FHIR-56387](https://jira.hl7.org/browse/FHIR-56387) (Reinhard: rename Patient Matching → Patient Lookup), and [FHIR-56398](https://jira.hl7.org/browse/FHIR-56398) (Charles: sex/gender/birthLocation as SHOULD). Filing as a separate Epic-voiced ticket reinforces the consensus that patient matching needs structural revision.

## Resolution

- Status: Resolved - change required
- Resolution: Not Persuasive with Modification
- Resolution Vote: Mattias Colliander / Charles Parisot: 6-0-1
- Vote Date: 2026-08-12

After a discussion on the API call the agreement was to keep the status quo, meaning that patient search is required and $match optional. Regarding the argument “…Identifier matching is necessary but not sufficient; demographics are needed alongside…” That depends on the governance for the identifier and the “set of agreed identification attributes” which is varying between the member states. Providing identifier and/or demographics can be accomplished with both patient search and $match transactions (reminder: SHALL support a certain search parameter means that a system has to able to process it and not that is has to be present in every query). Josh provided a use case from the EPIC system where patient lookup is also used for verification purposes, to achieve this as much patient data (identifier+demographics) as possible is needed. This can also be accomplished with a patient search transaction and described via layered CapabilityStatments. The use case and the explanation will be added to the patient lookup page.
