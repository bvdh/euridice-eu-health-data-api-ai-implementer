### Giorgio Cangioli — 2026-04-16

The text includes references to paragraph numbers (e.g., “satisfy §2.2”) that are automatically assigned by the publisher. These paragraph numbers are not stable and may change between versions, leading to broken or misleading references.

Replace references to paragraph numbers with stable references, such as:

	- Section titles (e.g., “Receive Data”)

	- Anchored hyperlinks to the corresponding section

	- Explicit identifiers if available
