# FHIR-56474: ePrescription and eDispensation

## Description

In paragraph 2.4.1.4 "Exchange Patterns" ([FHIR Documents and Resources - EU Health Data API v1.0.0-ballot](https://hl7.eu/fhir/health-data-api/1.0.0-ballot/en/fhir-documents-vs-resources.html))

The priority categories ePrescription and eDispensation are missing in the ressource access pattern. Allergies, conditions, immunizations, medications and observation are not in the list of the priority categories of the regulation.

## Resolution

- Status: Resolved - No Change
- Resolution: Considered - No action required

Reading prescription and dispense data is covered through resource access, including MedicationRequest, MedicationDispense and MedicationStatement. Resource access is not restricted to the six EHDS priority categories; individual resource types and priority-category documents are distinct concepts. Clarify this distinction while retaining prescribing and dispensing workflow transactions outside scope. Point readers to the [HL7 EU Medication Prescription and Dispense IHE Transactions page](https://hl7.eu/fhir/mpd/transactions.html) for informative workflow guidance. Further ePrescription/eDispensation API profiling may be addressed in a future dedicated implementation guide; no replacement of existing national or MyHealth@EU transactions is prescribed here.
