# FHIR-56368: Clarify SMART compliance with EU regulation

## Description

The Implementation Guide proposes the use of SMART-on-FHIR for authorization, but it is unclear whether this approach is fully compliant with applicable EU regulations, in particular:

	- eIDAS Regulation (electronic identification and trust services)

	- EU Digital Identity Wallet requirements

	- GDPR-related constraints on authentication and authorization

Add a clarification section describing how the SMART-on-FHIR approach aligns with EU regulatory requirements. This could include:

	- Mapping SMART authentication flows to eIDAS-compliant identity assurance levels

	- Explaining how EU Digital Identity Wallets can be integrated into the SMART authorization flow
