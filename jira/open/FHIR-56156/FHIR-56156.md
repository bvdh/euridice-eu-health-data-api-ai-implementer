# FHIR-56156: "Access provider" naming is confusing and will likely conflict with other actors in EHDS

## Description

The term "access provider" seems more about granting access, not the actual providing the content. If this is used for the latter purpose, what will happen with actors that are supposed to allow patient or their representatives to request and be granted access? Propose renaming these actors
