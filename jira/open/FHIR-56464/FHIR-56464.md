# FHIR-56464: Query-based exchange / point of care

## Description

In paragraph 2.1.0.5 "Xt-EHR Deliverable 5.1" ([Regulatory Anchors - EU Health Data API v1.0.0-ballot](https://hl7.eu/fhir/health-data-api/1.0.0-ballot/en/regulatoryAnchors.html)).

"Clinical workflow fit: When a patient arrives at a new care setting, their health data often resides elsewhere. Query-based exchange allows the treating provider to retrieve patient data at the point of care, rather than requiring precoordination with the data source to transmit the patient's data."

Health data can be retrieved directly at the point of care but there are also other methods to access health data. In Articles 11 and 12, Member States shall ensure that health professionals are able to access health data through health professional access services (HPAS). During the CapacityHD sessions "Preparing for the EEHRxF (Interoperability and Interoperability Testing) continued: Towards an EHDS Use Cases Catalogue for Primary Use" hold in Athens and Brussels, the participants (all from Member States) worked on a configuration where health data are accessed through the HPA Services provided by the countries. These services can be directly connected to the point of care, but many member states provide an infrastructure where health data are centralized in a national EHR and/or in regional EHRs and/or in local EHR systems. The description of the clinical workfow here is too restrictive and does not described the full reality. If the objective of this work is to be adopted by all Member States, the IG must include as much as possible the architectures already implemented. Please have in mind that countries will rely on their current infrastructure to implement EHDS. I suggest to change the sentence:

"Clinical workflow fit: When a patient arrives at a new care setting, their health data often resides elsewhere. Query-based exchange allows the treating provider to retrieve data from the Electronic Health Record of the patient, rather than requiring precoordination with the data source to transmit the patient's data."

I would also correct the following sentences:

"MyHealth@EU alignment: EU Cross-border services are primarily modeled as query-based exchanges. The requesting country's National Contact Point queries the responding National Contact Point, which queries the country's health data infractructure.

"Data availability: Healthcare is 24/7. An API endpoint can serve requests at any time, ensuring data is accessible when needed for care. With a push architecture, data availability depends on the receiver being online and the sender initiating data transmission."
