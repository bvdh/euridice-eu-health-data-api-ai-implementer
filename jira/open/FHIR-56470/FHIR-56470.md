# FHIR-56470: Interoperability requirements will be provided in IA of Article 36

## Description

In paragraph 2.1.0.8 "Requirement Tables" ([Regulatory Anchors - EU Health Data API v1.0.0-ballot](https://hl7.eu/fhir/health-data-api/1.0.0-ballot/en/regulatoryAnchors.html)).

"The following table maps each D5.1 interoperability requirement to its implementation in this IG."

Be aware that the source of interoperability requirements will be the implementing act of Article 36. Member states made a lot of comment in the first backgroud document sent by the EC last month. The list of requirements may change in the next few month. I suggest to rewrite the sentence:

"The list of requirements for the interoperability component will be provided in the Implementing Act of Article 36. Since the IA is not available yet, the following table maps each D5.1 interoperability requirement to its implementation in this IG."
