# FHIR-56462: Adoption by the Member States

## Description

In paragraph 2.1.0.2 "EHDS Regulation" ([Regulatory Anchors - EU Health Data API v1.0.0-ballot](https://hl7.eu/fhir/health-data-api/1.0.0-ballot/en/regulatoryAnchors.html)). 

« Note that this IG does NOT create legal obligations on EHR Systems unless adopted by the European Commission. ».

This IG should also be adopted by the Members States since they have sovereignty over the management of their healthcare systems (Article 168, point 7 and Article 153 point 4 of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union). I suggest correcting the sentence.

« Note that this IG does NOT create legal obligations on EHR Systems unless adopted by the European Commission and the Members States. ».
