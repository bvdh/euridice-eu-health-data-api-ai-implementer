# FHIR-56676: Section 4.1.1 Sequence Diagram: required vs optional transactions

## Description

Section 4.1.1 Sequence Diagram

The sequence diagram does not distinguish between mandatory and optional interactions, such as:

	- Retrieval of metadata (DocumentReference)

	- Patient lookup / identification steps

This may lead to the assumption that all steps are always required.

Proposed Change:

Enhance the diagram by:

	- - Clearly marking optional steps (e.g., using dashed lines, annotations, or notes)

 - Indicating conditions under which steps are performed
