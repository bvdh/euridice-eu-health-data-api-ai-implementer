# FHIR-56629: Allow separate PDF DocumentReference

## Description

Issue:

The statement:

“Human-readable representations (e.g. PDF narrative) are part of the FHIR Document as defined by the relevant content IG — not exposed at metadata level as separate DocumentReferences.”

is too restrictive and may conflict with common interoperability patterns:

	- Human-readable representations (e.g., PDF) are often exchanged as separate Binary resources

	- Dual-representation patterns (e.g., structured + PDF) using multiple DocumentReference resources are also used

Proposed Change:

Revise the statement to allow alternative patterns, e.g.:

“Human-readable representations (e.g. PDF) may be included within a FHIR Document as defined by the relevant content IG, or may be exposed as separate representations (e.g., Binary resources referenced by DocumentReference). In such cases, multiple DocumentReferences may be used to represent alternative formats of the same content.”
