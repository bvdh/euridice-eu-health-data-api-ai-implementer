# FHIR-56845: Revise actors and SMART references

## Description

The grouping descriptions for actors refer primarily to IHE actors, which may be misleading in the context of this Implementation Guide:

	- The guide defines EU-specific (EHDS) actors, which are not identical to IHE actors but rather constrained/specialized versions

	- Referencing IHE actors alone may suggest that conformance to IHE is sufficient, which is not the case

	- The inclusion of IUA Authorization Client is inconsistent with the rest of the guide, where SMART is stated as authoritative

	- SMART is implicitly treated as mandatory rather than a preferred option, which contradicts the need for flexibility in EU cross-border contexts

Proposed Change:

	- Revise actor descriptions to primarily describe EHDS-specific actors, and clarify their relationship to IHE actors, e.g.:

“These actors are defined by this guide and may align with or be based on IHE actors, but SHALL comply with the constraints defined herein.”

	- If IHE actors are referenced:

 - Explicitly state that conformance to IHE alone is not sufficient

 - Clarify that the IG defines additional constraints and requirements

	- Remove IUA Authorization Client from the grouping definitions

	- Clarify SMART positioning, e.g.:

“SMART-based authorization is the preferred approach in this guide but is not the only possible mechanism.”

## Resolution

- Status: Triaged
- Resolution: Persuasive with Modification

Clarify that the actors and groupings are defined by this IG and that conformance to an underlying IHE or SMART profile alone does not establish conformance to the IG’s additional requirements. Retain IUA groupings where selected by the specified environment and use case; describe SMART and IUA according to those selections rather than asserting a global SMART preference or mandate.
