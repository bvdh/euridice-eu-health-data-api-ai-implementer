# FHIR-56653: Don't mandate locale-specific CapabilityStatements where the underlying capability has utility outside the EU

## Description

A truly international FHIR server could easily have a dozen or more distinct IG-mandated CapabilityStatements per locale. Imagine the bloat and size of a CapabilityStatement resource in this scenario. Strive to require capability statements that are relevant in different regulatory locales when the referenced interop capabilities are possibly usable in those distinct locales.

Proposed resolution:

Don't mandate EU-locked CapabilityStatement structure where the underlying capability (e.g., a queryable patient summary) is also valuable outside the EU. Where per-locale conformance is genuinely required, document the rationale; otherwise let servers express the same capabilities under universal-realm IGs alongside or instead of the EU-specific ones. Pairs with the don't-EU-lock-value-sets ticket below.
