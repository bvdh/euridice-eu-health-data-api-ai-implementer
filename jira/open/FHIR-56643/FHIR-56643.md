# FHIR-56643: Informative vs normative content

## Description

The guide does not provide clear guidance on how to interpret its different sections. It is not always clear which parts are:

	- Normative (required for conformance)

	- Informative (guidance or examples)

This may lead to inconsistent interpretation by implementers regarding what is mandatory versus optional.

Proposed Change:

Add a dedicated section (e.g., “How to Read This Guide”) that Explains the structure of the guide indicating what is normative and what is informatve

and/or

Clearly distinguishes between normative requirements and informative content in the pages
