# FHIR-56677: revise Section 4.1.5.1 Step 1: Discover Capabilities

## Description

Section 4.1.5.1 Step 1: Discover Capabilities

1)

The sentence:

“The CapabilityStatement confirms support for IHE MHD document exchange, PDQm patient search, and European Patient Summary priority category”

is misleading because it suggests checking support for specific IHE transactions rather than verifying conformance to the profiles and capabilities defined in this Implementation Guide.

2)

The step “Discover Capabilities” is presented as part of the sequence without clarifying whether it is mandatory or optional. In practice, capability discovery is often not performed at runtime and may be done out-of-band.
