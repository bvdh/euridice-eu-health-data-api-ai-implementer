# FHIR-56615: SMART should be preferred not mandatory

## Description

The Implementation Guide proposes the use of SMART-on-FHIR for authorization, but it is unclear whether this approach is fully compliant with applicable EU regulations, in particular:

	- eIDAS Regulation (electronic identification and trust services)

	- EU Digital Identity Wallet requirements

	- GDPR-related constraints on authentication and authorization

 

Consider to add a clarification section describing how the SMART-on-FHIR approach aligns with EU regulatory requirements. This could include:

	- Mapping SMART authentication flows to eIDAS-compliant identity assurance levels

	- Explaining how EU Digital Identity Wallets can be integrated into the SMART authorization flow

	- Clarifying any constraints or required adaptations for GDPR compliance

 

Suggest in any case to consider SMART not a mandatory solution.

## Resolution

- Status: Triaged
- Resolution: Persuasive with Modification

The related authorization changes are in [PR #99 — Eu health data api ballot recon authorization](#99 — Eu health data api ballot recon authorization)([https://github.com/euridice-org/eu-health-data-api/pull/99](https://github.com/euridice-org/eu-health-data-api/pull/99)). eIDAS assurance-level mapping The authorization page describes eIDAS-recognised authentication in relevant interactive flows and references eIDAS Article 8 and Implementing Regulation (EU) 2015/1502. Those provisions define assurance-level criteria for electronic identification schemes; the supplied EHDS analysis does not establish which level, if any, applies to a particular EHDS service, data type, or SMART flow, and identifies its EHDS review as provisional. This resolution therefore assigns and recommends no assurance level. A level should not be stated until the applicable EHDS, other EU, and national requirements have been verified. European Digital Identity Wallet integration The authorization page says a Wallet may be used as an authentication means and that OpenID for Verifiable Presentations may be used, but the IG selects no presentation format, credential schema, or Wallet-to-Authorization-Server profile. This resolution records that Wallet use is possible within the described authentication context, without selecting or implying a standardized Wallet integration. GDPR constraints and adaptations The current working-tree source distinguishes technical authentication/API authorization from the GDPR legal basis and Article 9(2) condition, states that HDAS service preferences are separate from the processing legal basis and that the IG does not define it, and says technical actor roles are not intended to determine GDPR controller/processor status. These concise statements are informative: they do not set a lawful basis, condition, party-role allocation, consent workflow, or SMART protocol requirement. They are uncommitted and are not included in PR #99's current head. The IG still does not provide detailed GDPR-specific constraints or adaptations for SMART; further guidance should be separately scoped and supported by applicable authority.
