# FHIR-56708: Revise Section 4.1.6

## Description

Section 4.1.6 .

The current sentence:

“FHIR Documents (IPS, etc.) are retrieved as Bundle resources; DICOM KOS imaging manifests (IHE MADO) are retrieved as Binary resources”

does not consider FHIR-based imaging manifest representations.

Proposed Change:

Update the sentence to include FHIR ImagingStudy, e.g.:

“FHIR Documents (IPS, etc.) are retrieved as Bundle resources; DICOM KOS imaging manifests (IHE MADO) are retrieved as Binary resources; FHIR imaging manifests (IHE MADO) are retrieved as ImagingStudy resources.”
