# FHIR-56451: Xt-EHR vs Implementing Act

## Description

In paragraph 1 "Home" ([Home - EU Health Data API v1.0.0-ballot](https://hl7.eu/fhir/health-data-api/1.0.0-ballot/en/index.html))

This IG is based on the deliverables of the joint action projet Xt-EHR and not the implementing acts of Article 15 and 36. A clear and big advertisment should be shown to the readers and warn them that this IG is a draft of the technical implementation of EHDS. The implementing are expected to be published by the end of the year and a new IG will be submitted.
