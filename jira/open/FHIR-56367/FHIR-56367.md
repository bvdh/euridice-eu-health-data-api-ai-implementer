# FHIR-56367: Add eIDAS/EU Wallet note (Access Provider)

## Description

The requirements for access do not explicitly reference compliance with EU regulations on electronic identification and trust services, nor the emerging EU Digital Identity Wallet requirements.

Add a note to the table or in relevant section, e.g.:

“Access mechanisms SHALL comply with the eIDAS Regulation and, where applicable, the EU Digital Identity Wallet requirements.”

Optionally include references to the relevant regulations or specifications.

## Resolution

- Status: Triaged
- Resolution: Persuasive with Modification

This ticket is addressed by the authorization clarifications in [PR #99](https://github.com/euridice-org/eu-health-data-api/pull/99). The Authorization section explains that, where the selected environment requires support for eIDAS-recognized electronic identification, the Authorization Server SHALL support user authentication using those means, directly or through an identity provider. This applies to interactive SMART or IUA authorization-code flows, not pre-authorized SMART Backend Services using client_credentials. It also clarifies that a Wallet may be used where applicable, without requiring every deployment to use one or defining a Wallet integration profile. The requirement is expressed at the Authorization Server and environment level rather than as a universal obligation on Access Providers. The Access Provider descriptions identify the applicable IUA or SMART Resource Server role and point to the environment-specific authorization requirements. This addresses the ticket’s intent while avoiding an overbroad eIDAS or Wallet requirement for deployments outside the EHDS access-service scope.

## Comments

### KatieReynolds — 2026-05-13

I agree that the IG should clarify the relationship with eIDAS and EU Digital Identity Wallet. However, I don't think the suggested wording is quite right.

EHDS doesn't mention eIDAS or EUDI Wallet as requirements for EHR vendors, only for national data access services and cross-border exchange.

I'd suggest something like:

EHDS Articles 12 and 16 specify that access mechanisms for health professional access services and for natural persons' access to electronic health data access services SHALL support electronic identification means recognized under Article 6 of Regulation (EU) No 910/2014 (eIDAS), with additional requirements for cross-border exchange to be defined via implementing acts under Article 16(2). Digital proxy solutions should also align (EHD Recital 21). Although not a direct eIDAS/EUDI Wallet conformance requirement on EHR vendors as part of the interoperability component, these requirements will be important for the implementation of EHDS and will be required of some EHRs (those used for these services).

The Implementing Acts for these requirements are not yet finalized, and as noted in [3.2.9 Potential Future Work: User-Level Authorization](https://build.fhir.org/ig/euridice-org/eu-health-data-api/en/authorization.html#potential-future-work-user-level-authorization), may be addressed in a future version of this IG.
