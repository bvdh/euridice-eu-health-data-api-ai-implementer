# FHIR-56624: Add ImagingStudy support

## Description

Section 3.4.3.1 Sequence Diagram

The sequence diagram appears to imply that retrieval of an image manifest is limited to DICOM KOS. This is too restrictive. An imaging manifest may also be represented as a FHIR ImagingStudy.

 

Proposed Change:

Update the sequence diagram to show that image manifest retrieval is not limited to DICOM KOS and may also return a FHIR ImagingStudy representation where applicable.
